# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want git-panic to be welcoming, respectful, and useful to people with
different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience.

Participation requires treating others with dignity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas, not personal worth.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and consent.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Follow maintainer decisions about project scope and moderation.

Disagreement is welcome when expressed professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks or repeated hostile interactions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or evasion of moderation.
- Retaliation against good-faith reporters.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project-managed spaces.

It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the repository owner, DudeSquad999.

For private reports, use a private contact method explicitly published by
the maintainer. If none is available, request a private contact method
without posting incident details publicly.

Do not publish private evidence, identifying information, or sensitive
allegations in a public issue.

This policy does not establish an emergency service or promise a response
time. For immediate threats, contact appropriate local emergency services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, close
conversations, or block accounts.

Depending on severity and context, responses may include:

1. A request to correct behavior.
2. A formal warning.
3. A temporary participation restriction.
4. A permanent participation restriction.

Serious misconduct may warrant an immediate restriction.

Maintainers should explain decisions when appropriate while protecting
reporters' privacy and safety.

## Appeals and Conflicts of Interest

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration
through the same reporting route and provide relevant additional context.

A maintainer directly involved in an incident should seek an uninvolved
reviewer where one is available.

The project does not promise an independent appeals body.

## Privacy

Handle reports with care. Share information only as needed to assess and
address the concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
